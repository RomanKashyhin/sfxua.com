    How we work.

    Your audio team, from first brief to in-game delivery.

    For the length of your project we plug into your team as its sound department. One SoundFX manager runs our side, so you never have to hand out tasks to each of our specialists.

    • Works in your tools Asana, Trello or the tracker you already use.
    • Checked in the game We play your build and listen, not just send files.
    • A month of support after every package we hand over.
    1. 1

      Step 1: Brief and scope

      We get to know your game, the tasks, the references and where the sound is today.

      Together we agree on the package: scope, priorities, timing and the result you expect.

    2. 2

      Step 2: We plug into your team

      Our manager joins your workspace: Asana, Trello or whatever you already use.

      We agree on owners, channels and approvals and fit into your process. No extra system just for us.

    3. 3

      Step 3: We run our tasks

      The manager logs requirements, assigns the work, updates statuses and collects questions.

      They talk to your programmers directly. You see clear progress and get results to approve.

    4. 4

      Step 4: We make the sound and bring it into the game

      We create the sound in the agreed package and work with your developers on how it is hooked up and behaves in the game.

      How far we go into implementation depends on what we agree and the access on your project.

    5. 5

      Step 5: We play, listen and fix

      Our specialists check the sound in the game itself: the available scenarios, transitions, repeats, loudness and timing against the action.

      We log issues, give feedback and fix what falls within the agreed work.

    6. 6

      Step 6: Handover and a month of support

      You get the agreed files and the notes your team needs to carry on.

      1 month of support

      After every package, on request, we fix bugs and make small adjustments to that package free of charge. New tasks or a bigger scope are agreed separately.

      Then our part ends cleanly: the files, the knowledge and the control stay with you.

      Cartoon worlds need sound that commits.

      SoundFX makes sound for games with cartoon and stylized art. We treat sound as part of character design: it adds weight to a squash, spring to a jump and attitude to a blink.

      • Pick a surface

        Match the shape

        Round, bouncy art calls for round, bouncy sound. We design from the silhouette and the animation, not from a generic library tone.

      • Play meClick or tap the keys. With the keyboard, use A to L for white keys and W, E, T, Y, U, O, P for black keys.

        Readable in the chaos

        Every gameplay sound has a job: tell the player what just happened. It should stay clear when ten things happen at once.

      • Sweep across the stringsPress Play, then pluck each string as its note reaches the target: pass the cursor across the string (no click needed), swipe across it on a touch screen, or use the keys D, F, J and K. Out of a game the strings can be plucked freely.

        One world, one voice

        Characters, UI and ambiences share a sonic palette, so the whole game sounds like it belongs together.

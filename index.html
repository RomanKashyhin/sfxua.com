How we work.

Working sound, without building an audio department.

Your team shares the context of the game. We tailor the sound, connect it to your game and check the result, with one manager running our side.

    01 Analysis & agreement

    We get the context of your game and agree on the character of the sound, the scope and the access we need.

    • A build, a video or a description of the game
    • The character of the sound and the agreed scope
    • Access to the project and the materials

    The result: the direction of your game’s sound world.

    02 Assembly & adaptation

    We create the sound effects and the music for your game and tune how they behave. Pick a sound category next to the scene to see it join the world.

    03 Integration & testing

    We connect the sound to your game and check how it behaves in context, then adjust the behavior and the balance.

    04 Delivery & support

    We hand over the ready solution with the materials and the notes your team needs.

    A month of support after handover We fix bugs and make small adjustments within the agreed integration package. New tasks are agreed separately.

        • Only the needed parts are connected and go into the build.

        The ambient module follows our product specification. What is available for your project today, we confirm in the audio plan.

        For developers: under the hood

        The ambient module is split into Runtime (shared behaviour and rules), Base, Add‑ons, Themes and Project settings. The whole system is designed around these principles of adaptive game audio:

        • Event-driven playback: game events start the sound.
        • Variations and repeat limits: the same action doesn’t sound identical every time.
        • Layers: a sound is built from parts that change separately.
        • States and materials: the sound follows surfaces, materials and game states.
        • Parameter control: game values steer the sound.
        • Smooth transitions: between places, states and music.
        • Spatial sound: sources sit in the world around the listener.
        • Mixing and priorities: important sounds stay audible.
        • Voice limits: a cap on how many sources play at once.
        • Platform tuning: import settings and budgets per target platform.

        Unity is the engine we support. The system runs inside your project; it isn’t FMOD or Wwise and doesn’t replace them. Which of these features your configuration uses is listed in the audio plan, and every connection is checked in your build.

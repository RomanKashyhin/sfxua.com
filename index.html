    How we work.

    Your audio team, from first brief to in‑game delivery.

    For the length of your project we join your team as its sound department, with one manager running our side.

    1. 01

      Step 1: Brief & onboarding

      • We take your request.
      • We agree on the tasks, the scope and the timing.
      • Our manager joins your Asana, Trello or other workspace.

    2. 02

      Step 2: Creation & coordination

      Cartoon worlds need sound that commits. This is where we make it.

      Round, bouncy art calls for round, bouncy sound. We design from the setting and the animation, not from a generic library tone.

      Coordination. Our manager runs our team’s tasks and works with your developers.

      1. 02.1 Sound effects & footsteps

        Pick a surface

        Every surface has its own step. Pick one and listen.

      2. 02.2 Music

        Play meClick or tap the keys. With the keyboard, use A to L for white keys and W, E, T, Y, U, O, P for black keys.

        The musical side of the work. Play the keys.

    3. 03

      Step 3: Integration & in‑game testing

      We check the sound and the music in the game itself: we work with your developers on hooking it up, play the available build, log notes and fix them.

      The little rhythm game on the screen only pictures this step: catch the notes on the strings.

      Sweep across the stringsPress Play, then pluck each string as its note reaches the target: pass the cursor across the string (no click needed), swipe across it on a touch screen, or use the keys D, F, J and K. Out of a game the strings can be plucked freely.

    4. 04

      Step 4: Delivery & support

      We hand over the agreed package and the notes your team needs.

      Support after handover for every package: we fix bugs and make small adjustments to it free of charge. New tasks are agreed separately.

    Studios we’ve worked with

      Sound with a job to do.

      We design sound for games with cartoon and stylized art. Each part of the game has its own job, so we design for how it plays, not only how it sounds.

      • Hup!

        Characters & creatures

        For the game
        Voices, efforts and reactions that tell players who a character is before they read a line of dialogue.
        For your team
        A vocal set per character, with enough variations for repeated actions like jumps, hits and cheers.

      • Bonk!

        Actions, impacts & abilities

        For the game
        Hits that land, jumps that spring and abilities that sound as strong as they look.
        For your team
        Layered sounds with variations, so actions that repeat a hundred times stay fresh.

      • Ding!

        UI, rewards & feedback

        For the game
        Taps, pickups and rewards that confirm every choice and make progress feel good.
        For your team
        One UI sound family: clicks, confirms, errors and celebrations that belong together.

      • Shhh…

        Ambiences & environments

        For the game
        Places that feel alive around the player: weather, machines, crowds and small creatures.
        For your team
        Loopable beds plus one-shot details you can layer per area.
